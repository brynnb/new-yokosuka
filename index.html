New Yokosuka

New Yokosuka is an unofficial Shenmue MMO experience. Based on the 1999 Sega Dreamcast classic Shenmue, it is playable entirely in your browser—no installation required. Explore the original world of New Yokosuka with other players, race forklifts, work at the harbor, engage in combat, play arcade games, talk to NPCs, and collect vending-machine toys.

The project has two main components: multiplayer world exploration and a general asset viewer and downloader. All assets were reverse engineered from the original Dreamcast game discs, building on existing projects alongside substantial new original work.

This is a fan-made project created by me, Brynn, and I’d love to hear your feedback in the Discord.

Credits & Resources

New Yokosuka builds on years of Shenmue community research and open-source tooling.

  • Tom Bowen - combat animation data.
  • Shenmue Export Tools — technical documentation and extraction research.
  • Wulinshu Wiki — Shenmue format documentation and data-structure references, Shenmue II item names, map names, and Shenmue II music filename-to-title mappings, based on Nameless Legacy’s spreadsheet and wiki contributors’ research.
  • Wudecon — format research notes and decoding logic.
  • gditools3 — GD-ROM extraction tooling.
  • EmulatorJS — browser-based emulation used by the playable arcade cabinets.
  • FinalBurn Neo — arcade hardware emulation powering Hang-On and Space Harrier.

And, of course, thanks to Shenmue’s original developers.
